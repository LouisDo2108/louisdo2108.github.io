Week 46 · 9–15 Nov 2026

5 new papers · 3 to revise · Mon 09 Nov – Sun 15 Nov 2026

Curated weekly with AI assistance. Summaries can contain mistakes, so check the paper before citing. Figures are reproduced for review with credit to the authors; contact me to have one removed.

trend of the week — Post-training is the common thread this week: theory explains why REINFORCE-style updates can collapse a base model's coverage, while self-distillation and RL in simulation push new knowledge and skills (even robot control) into open-weight models. On the retrieval side, LLM-written natural-language interest profiles are now a production retrieval key at Meta, and world models increasingly reuse pretrained video encoders such as V-JEPA 2 instead of learning actions from scratch.

arXiv 2026

CLEAR: Legible and Steerable Recommendation via Grounded Natural-Language Interests

Meta

arXiv preprint

A deployed recommender (Instagram, Facebook) whose user state is an editable list of LLM-inferred, behaviour-grounded interests; each interest drives lexical, behavioural and embedding retrieval plus ranking.

Agents & agentic RL

arXiv 2026

Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots

MIT, UC Berkeley, UCSD, NVIDIA

arXiv preprint

Treats robot control like computer use: an open-weight 27B reasoning model is trained with RL purely in simulation to output end-effector poses, then transfers to unseen tasks, a new robot and a real dual-arm robot.

Reinforcement learning

arXiv 2026

A Theory of Policy Optimization in Post-Training: Coverage Collapse and Reference Resets

Microsoft Research, Kempner Institute at Harvard, UIUC, UCSD

arXiv preprint

Shows REINFORCE (and so PPO/GRPO) can drive the probability of optimal responses towards zero even as reward rises, and proves that resetting the KL reference to the latest policy restores convergence.

use it now → In long GRPO/PPO runs, periodically reset the KL reference model to the current policy instead of keeping the base model fixed.

pdfarxiv
arXiv 2026

Speculative Self-Distillation enables Efficient Knowledge Internalization

Stanford, ETH Zürich

arXiv preprint

Internalizes new facts and skills by self-distillation where the document-conditioned teacher only intervenes on tokens where it disagrees with the student; near on-policy accuracy with 38–69% fewer supervised tokens and the least forgetting.

use it now → To bake updated documents into a model closed-book, let the student roll out and only take teacher tokens where the two next-token distributions diverge strongly.

World models

arXiv 2026

OnlyAct: Extracting Action Representations from Foundation Models for World Models

University of Oxford

arXiv preprint

Instead of learning latent actions from scratch, adapts V-JEPA 2 with label-free contrastive training so frame-pair embeddings keep the motion and drop the scene, giving one action space that transfers across domains.
