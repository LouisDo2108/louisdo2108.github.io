// the problem, in one picture

Why continual retrieval is harder than classification

task t · encoder after training

Squares are image embeddings, circles are captions, and colours are concepts. When the encoder is fine-tuned on task t+1 (new data: strawberries), old embeddings drift; faint ghosts mark where they were. They stay inside their class regions, so classification accuracy holds. But nearest neighbours reshuffle, the right caption leaves the top-k, and Recall@1 collapses. Retrieval needs methods that preserve the geometry of the space, not just the labels.
